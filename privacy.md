# Say It: Manifest it — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 12 November 2026

Say It: Manifest it (shown on your phone as "Say It") is a daily affirmations app. This policy explains what information the app uses, where that information is
kept, and the choices you have.

**The short version:**

- **Your practice stays on your phone.** Your affirmations, sets, reminders and history are stored only on your device.
  We do not have a server that receives them, and we cannot see them. The text you write is encrypted on your phone.
- **The app never uses your microphone.** It reads affirmations aloud with your phone's built-in voice. It does not
  listen, record or send audio anywhere.
- **No account, no ads, no tracking.** You don't sign up, we don't show ads in the app, and we don't track you across
  other apps or websites.
- **We don't sell or share your personal information.**
- **Purchases are handled by Apple.** If you subscribe, Apple processes the payment. A payment service we use,
  RevenueCat, confirms your subscription using an anonymous ID. Neither we nor RevenueCat receive your name, email or
  card details.

## 2. Information that stays on your device

The app stores the following **only on your device**, so it can do its job. We never receive this information.

| What | Why the app needs it |
|---|---|
| Topics you choose during setup | To suggest affirmations that fit you |
| Affirmations and sets you create or save, including any you write yourself | So you can practise them |
| Your reminder times, days and the "anchor" sentence you write (e.g. "After I make my coffee…") | To schedule your reminders and show your plan |
| A log of when reminders were scheduled, delivered and opened | To check that reminders really arrive, and to show you if they don't |
| Your practice history: sessions, repetitions and timing | To count your repetitions and show your progress |
| Your settings (sound, voice, reminders, time zone, language) and the date your free period started | To run the app the way you set it up |
| A random ID for this install, kept in your phone's secure storage | To tell one phone's error reports apart, but only if you turn crash reports on (see section 3) |

**Voice and microphone.** Say It reads your affirmations aloud with your phone's built-in voice. The app never uses your
microphone, never records audio and does not use speech recognition. It does not ask for microphone or speech
permissions.

**Encryption.** The text you write (your own affirmations, set names and the "anchor" sentences) is encrypted on your
phone. The key is kept in your phone's secure storage (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore), not with the data. Things
that are not text you wrote, such as times, counts and settings, are stored without this extra encryption, inside the
app's private storage on your phone.

**Notifications.** Reminders are scheduled on your phone by the operating system. They aren't sent from a server. By
default a reminder shows a general message and none of your own words. If you switch on "Show my own affirmation and
anchor sentence in reminders" (Reminders screen), a reminder can show that text, which may be visible on your lock
screen. You can switch it off again at any time, and you can change what the app may show in your phone's notification
settings.

**Device backups.** On iPhone, Say It's data may be included in your iCloud or computer backup if you have backups turned
on. Your text stays unreadable without the key, and the key is only included in an encrypted iPhone backup. On Android,
Say It turns Android's automatic backup off, so a new phone starts fresh. Backups are controlled by you and your
phone's maker, under their privacy policies.

## 3. Information that leaves your device

### Purchases (RevenueCat and Apple)

If you buy a subscription or restore a purchase:

- **Apple** processes the payment using your Apple Account. We never see your payment details. See Apple's privacy
  policy: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>.
- **RevenueCat, Inc.** is our subscription service. It receives a random, anonymous app user ID, your purchase and
  subscription history from Apple, and your store country and currency. We use this only to check whether you have an
  active subscription, to restore purchases, and to see total subscription numbers. This ID is not linked to your name,
  email or any other account. See RevenueCat's privacy policy: <https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy>.

**Legal basis (GDPR / UK GDPR):** performance of our contract with you (providing the subscription), and our legitimate
interest in understanding subscription numbers.

### Crash reports (optional, off unless you turn them on)

After you finish setting up, Say It asks once whether you want to share crash reports. You can change your answer at any
time in Settings → Share crash reports. If you say no, which is the default, nothing is sent.

If you say yes, when the app breaks it sends a report to our error-tracking service, Sentry
(<https://sentry.io/privacy/>), stored in the European Union (Germany). A report contains: the error and the
technical steps that led to it, the app version and build, your iPhone or Android model and system version, the time, and
a random ID the app made for this install. The ID is not your name, email, device ID, advertising ID or purchase, and it
cannot be used to find you. A report never contains your affirmations, set names, reminder text, topics, email, phone
number, name, location or IP address, and the app removes any such text before sending. We use reports only to find and
fix problems. We do not use them for advertising or tracking.

Sentry keeps reports for up to 30 days. "Delete my data" in Settings turns crash reports off and replaces the random
ID, so reports sent earlier can no longer be tied to your phone, but it cannot recall reports that were already sent;
they expire on their own.

**Legal basis (GDPR / UK GDPR):** your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings.

### Information Apple shares with developers

If you have chosen in your iPhone settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous crash
reports and usage statistics (for example, how many people opened the app). Apple strips this of anything that
identifies you. You control it in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

### Links to other websites

The app links to outside websites, such as crisis helplines and Apple's subscription settings. Those sites have their
own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for them.

### What we don't do

We don't use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs or tracking pixels in the app. The only service of this kind is the
optional crash reporting described above, which is off unless you turn it on. We don't use your device's advertising
identifier. We don't sell or share personal information, as those terms are defined in the
California Consumer Privacy Act, and we don't use it for targeted advertising.

## 4. How long information is kept

- **On your device:** until you delete it in the app or delete the app.
- **RevenueCat:** for as long as needed to manage your subscription and meet tax and accounting obligations, and then
  deleted, or deleted earlier if you ask us (see section 5).
- **Emails you send us:** for as long as needed to answer you, and no more than 24 months after.

## 5. Your choices and rights

**Seeing and taking a copy of your data.** In Settings → **Export my data**, Say It makes a readable file of what is on
your phone (your own affirmations and sets, reminder settings and anchor sentences, practice history, reminder history,
settings and the date your free period started) and opens your phone's share sheet, so you choose where it goes. The
file is not sent to us, and the temporary copy is deleted after you share it.

**Deleting your data.** Because your practice data is only on your phone, you are in full control of it. In Settings →
**Delete my data** (after you confirm), Say It erases your affirmations, sets, reminders, practice history and settings
from the phone, replaces the encryption key so that any leftover copy can never be read, cancels your reminders,
switches crash reports off, replaces the random install ID, and takes you back to the welcome screen. Deleting the Say
It app removes your practice data from your device, but iOS can keep the random install ID and the encryption key in your
phone's secure storage (the Keychain) after the app is deleted. "Delete my data" replaces both, so use it before you delete
the app if you want a clean break. Say It keeps the date your free period started and your subscription
status after "Delete my data", because erasing them would restart the free period; they are not personal information.
"Delete my data" cannot recall crash reports that were already sent (see section 3).

To have your purchase record deleted from RevenueCat, email us. We'll ask you for the order ID from your Apple purchase
receipt so we can find the record, and we'll confirm once it's done.

Deleting your data does **not** cancel your subscription. To cancel, go to iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

**Your rights.** Depending on where you live (including the EU, UK, California and other US states, Australia and
India), you may have the right to:

- access the personal information we hold about you;
- correct it;
- delete it;
- receive a copy in a portable format;
- object to or restrict how we use it;
- withdraw any consent you've given;
- nominate someone to exercise your rights for you, where the law allows;
- complain to your local data-protection authority, for example the ICO in the UK, your EU supervisory authority, the
  OAIC in Australia, or the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, email **synaptic.habit@gmail.com**. We'll reply within one month, or sooner if your local law
requires it, and we won't treat you differently for using your rights. Since we hold almost nothing about you, in most
cases the answer will be what's described in section 3.

**Permissions.** Notification access is the only permission the app asks for, and only so it can show your reminders. You
can turn it on or off at any time in your phone's Settings. The app keeps working without it.

## 6. Children

Say It is not made for children and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal
information from children. Because the app keeps your practice data on your device and never sends it to us, we don't
receive it from anyone, of any age. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email,
contact us and we'll delete it.

## 7. Security

Your practice data is protected by your phone's own security, including your passcode and the encryption the operating
system applies to app data, and the text you write has its own encryption on top (section 2). Purchase information and
crash reports (if you turn them on) are sent over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the
information we handle to the minimum so there is very little that could be exposed.

## 8. International transfers

RevenueCat and Apple may process purchase information in the United States and other countries. Where the law
requires it, those transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard
Contractual Clauses.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change is significant, for example if the app
starts sending new information off your device, we'll tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
